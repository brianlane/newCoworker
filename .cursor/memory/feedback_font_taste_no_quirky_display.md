Brian rejected Space Grotesk as the marketing/heading font minutes after it deployed ("The new font looks very bad. Research online to find a better font"). Replaced with Geist headings + Inter body (PR #1663, Aug 27 2026).

**Why:** Space Grotesk's quirky, techy letterforms read off-brand for New Coworker. The brand wants trustworthy-professional, not standing-out-clever.

**How to apply:** For any New Coworker visual/typography choice, default to neutral, premium, widely-proven faces (Geist, Inter; system-adjacent metrics). Do not introduce characterful display fonts (Space Grotesk, Clash Display, and similar) without showing Brian a preview first. This likely generalizes to other design flourishes: restrained beats distinctive.
