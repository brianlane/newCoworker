Facts established 2026-08-01 while tracing the Telnyx auto-reload (PRs #1110, #1118):

- `/v2/detail_records` has three pagination quirks (PRs #1118, #1120, #1123): (1) responses clamp to 50 rows regardless of `page[size]`, so a short page is NOT the last page; trust `meta.total_pages`. (2) The REQUESTED `page[size]` multiplies into a 10,000-record offset ceiling (error 10011), so requesting 250 walls the pull at page 41; always request `page[size]=50`, giving 200 reachable pages. A window past 10k records must be pulled as narrower ranges. (3) Long pulls hit a windowed rate limiter; pace ~350ms between pages and back off 429s up to 60s. Before the fixes every sync kept only the newest ~50 records per type and the delete+reinsert erased older days.
- MDR `cost` ALREADY INCLUDES `carrier_fee`. Summing cost + carrier_fee double-counts; `telnyx_cost_daily.cost_micros` alone is the true total, `carrier_fee_micros` is informational.
- Ground truth for reconciliation: `GET /v2/invoices` then `GET /v2/invoices/{id}?action=link` returns `download_url` (presigned PDF). A correctly paginated MDR pull matches the invoice messaging subtotal to the cent (June 2026: $6.84).
- Recurring charges never appear in detail_records: number MRC ($1.00) + SMS feature MRC ($0.10) post on the 1st of the month per DID; the 10DLC campaign fee ($10.00/mo, one shared CUSTOMER_CARE campaign for the whole platform) posts on the 6th. Number activations happen at purchase.
- Balance mechanics: `GET /v2/balance` (live, never persisted), `GET /v2/payment/auto_recharge_prefs` (threshold $2.00, recharge $28.00 via PayPal as of Aug 2026). There is NO public payments/ledger/charges API (404); exact payment amounts live only in the emailed receipts and the portal.
- RATE DECK (found Aug 5 2026, corrects the earlier "no rate API" note): `https://telnyx.com/pricing.md` is Telnyx's machine-readable deck (~810KB markdown) with per-country long-code SMS termination sell prices under `### Sms Api`, cost-code rows like `HK-LC-SMS-RATE0-TERMINATION`. 217 countries; 196 at the $0.004 floor (incl. Hong Kong); expensive tail is EU/LATAM (DK $0.16 ... MX $0.091 ... UK $0.055). The authenticated `GET /v2/pricing` returns ONLY phone-number + SIP rates (CSV), no messaging. Still unverified from the deck alone: whether international long-code bills per message or per part (rows say "per message"; our MX math assumed per part) : settle with one live send + MDR readback.
- Scale reference: July 2026 true usage $30.78 (2,027 msgs; Amy Laidlaw $20.30), June $6.98, vs $0.42 the broken sync had recorded for July.
- GROUP-MMS LOOKUP ARTIFACT (found Aug 10 2026): `GET /v2/messages/{id}` returns 404 for group-MMS message ids (e.g. Clever group-thread replies; all recipients share one id in `sms_outbound_log`), so `debug/trace-sms.ts` prints `carrier: http 404` and counts the send as "not confirmed delivered" even when it WAS delivered. Same-minute non-group sends resolve fine, so the 404 is per-message-type, not an API-key issue. Ground truth is `/v2/detail_records` (record_type=messaging): the MDR row carries `group_message_id`, per-leg `status` (`delivered`/`failed`/`dlr_unconfirmed`), and cost. Verified: an Aug 7 group reply that trace-sms flagged 404 was `delivered` to the lead's T-Mobile handset per MDR.

- TELNYX TAX IS INTRASTATE-VS-INTERSTATE, NOT USAGE-VS-MRC (verified against primary sources Aug 17 2026). Our model in enterprise-pricing.ts (TELNYX_USAGE_TAX_RATE 5.4% on usage, TELNYX_MRC_TAX_RATE 0.64% on MRCs) has the wrong SHAPE, and its two errors cancel at today's volume. A.R.S. 42-5064(A): "The telecommunications classification is comprised of the business of providing intrastate telecommunications services", and (E)(5) requires the traffic to "originate and terminate in this state" (it enumerates "messages, data", so SMS is NOT exempt: ADOR ruling TPR 04-1 Example 7 says text messaging is taxable). Cities are barred from taxing interstate telecom outright by A.R.S. 42-6004(A)(2). 42-5064(D)(1) lets the carrier apply an allocation percentage.
- Rates confirmed for Mesa AZ 85207 (ADOR TPT table effective Jul 1 2026, business code 005 Communications): AZ state 5.6% (5% under 42-5010(A)(1)(c) + 0.6% education increment under 42-5010.01, in force to 2041), Maricopa 0.7%, Mesa 2.0%, combined 8.3%. Mesa's telecom rate happens to equal its retail rate; other AZ cities differ (Marana taxes communications at 4.0% vs 2.5% retail), so do not generalize if the billing address moves.
- July 2026 reconciliation of the $1.94 of sales tax: implied taxable base $23.31-$23.48. Everything except messaging ($22.44: numbers, feature MRC, voice/data, 10DLC) appears taxed at 100%, plus only ~3% of the $30.36 messaging line, consistent with ~97% of A2P messages terminating outside Arizona. The 3% allocation is INFERRED from arithmetic, never confirmed by Telnyx: ask them for the per-line taxable base and their AZ intrastate allocation percentage before hard-coding it.
- FCC USF factor Q3 2026 = 0.388 (38.8%, an all-time high; unadjusted 0.387708, DA 26-546); Q2 2026 was 37.0%. It applies to interstate + international end-user TELECOM revenue only, and messaging is excluded federally as a Title I information service (FCC 18-178). Our $0.11 implies just $0.28 of assessable revenue, well under the 38.8% billing cap.
- Possibly missing from Telnyx's invoice: A.R.S. 42-5252(A) 911 levy ($0.20/month per activated account, ADOR code 911) and 42-5252(B) 1.1% hearing-impaired levy (code 033), both of which 42-5252(C) requires as separate invoice line items. Whether a CPaaS is a "provider" under 42-5251(5) is genuinely arguable, so this is a question for Telnyx, not established underbilling.
- Exhaustive check of all 328 published ADOR rulings: "text messaging" appears in exactly ONE (TPR 04-1, still in force, no rescission), and "SMS"/"short message service" appear in ZERO. Arizona has published nothing on A2P/CPaaS messaging. The 2019 over-the-top amendment (SB1019, ch. 189) does NOT reach messaging: ADOR deduction code 789 reads "Over the Top (OTT) Services - Radio or Television ONLY", and there is no deduction code for messaging/data/information services under business code 005. Model City Tax Code Sec. 470 was never repealed, only Regulation 470.1 (folded into Sec. 470, eff. Jul 1 2019); the ADOR URL for "telecommunication-services" 301-redirects to the REPEALED regulation page, so use the "-reg-4701" slug for live Sec. 470.
- FCC 18-178 (SMS/MMS are Title I information services) does NOT help in Arizona and says nothing about state taxation. Arizona's test is WHO TRANSMITS, not the Communications Act classification: Excell Agent Services v. ADOR (1 CA-TX 07-0003, 2008) held a directory-assistance provider was outside the telecom class because it "provides information that is transmitted, but it does not actually transmit" it. A CPaaS operates the transport, so it is inside. 42-5064(E)(5) also lacks the "without change in form or content" clause the federal test turns on.
- The resale exemption is NOT available to us: 42-5064(B)(1)(a) only exempts sales to persons themselves in the telecom classification, and under Excell a business buying messaging to text its own customers is an END USER.
- TWO candidate explanations for the untaxed messaging, and they carry different risk: (a) interstate sourcing (ADOR deduction code 643, Cable Plus Co. v. ADOR 197 Ariz. 507: the ORIGINATION POINT OF THE CONTENT governs) which is legitimate and stable, or (b) Telnyx taking an FCC information-service position, which Arizona law does not support and would leave back-tax exposure. Arithmetic discriminates: a blanket (b) would tax exactly $22.44 and yield $1.86-1.87, but the invoice charged $1.94, so a nonzero slice of messaging WAS taxed, pointing to (a). Twilio litigated the analogous CPaaS question against San Francisco and SETTLED for $18M, then resumed collecting on messaging from Feb 1 2024.
- Modeling caveat: the intrastate share is NOT a stable constant. It is the AZ-to-AZ fraction of fleet traffic, so it shrinks as non-Arizona tenants (KYP Ads +1438 Montreal, Truly Insurance +1519 Ontario, Scar Fairy +1305 Miami) grow relative to Amy's Phoenix-area traffic.
